Miner Simulator is a fun 2D clicker where you play as a regular miner and earn ore!

Features:
Resource Mining: Click to extract valuable ores and grow your capital.
Upgrades: Purchase better equipment and tools to boost your mining efficiency.
Atmospheric Audio: Immersive background music and satisfying sound effects.
Simple & Intuitive: Easy-to-understand controls — jump right into the action!
Download the installer, run the game, and see how fast you can grow your mine!
